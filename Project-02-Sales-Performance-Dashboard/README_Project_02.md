# Sales Performance Analysis & Dashboard — Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a sales dataset using Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue performance across regions, products, categories, and months. The workbook includes data preparation, validation documentation, analysis tables, a dashboard, and business insights with recommendations.

## Objectives

- Prepare and validate sales data for analysis.
- Analyze revenue performance across regions, products, categories, and months.
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Present findings in an interactive-style Excel dashboard using charts and summary metrics.
- Translate the analysis into actionable business questions and recommendations.

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and data-cleaning techniques
- PivotTables and PivotCharts / charts
- Data validation and reconciliation checks

## Workbook Structure

| Worksheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Raw_Data` | Original dataset retained for reference. |
| `Cleaned_Data` | Prepared data used for analysis. |
| `Inspection_Log` | Documents data-quality issues, actions taken, and validation notes. |
| `Analysis` | Supporting calculations and analysis tables. |
| `PivotTables` | Summarized sales metrics used to support dashboard charts and insights. |
| `Dashboard` | KPI cards and charts summarizing sales performance. |
| `Business Insights` | Findings, evidence, business implications, and recommendations. |

## Data Preparation

Data preparation included:

- Standardizing date formats.
- Removing extra spaces from text fields.
- Standardizing inconsistent region text (for example, capitalization differences).
- Removing duplicate Order IDs from the cleaned dataset.
- Reviewing missing values.
- Reconstructing missing Quantity or Unit Price values mathematically where possible, using Sales Amount and the other available field.
- Validating sales calculations using Quantity × Unit Price = Sales Amount.

Blank region values were retained because their correct region could not be verified. Reconstructed numeric values are derived and should be verified against the original source if available.

## Analysis

The project examines:

- **Regional revenue:** compares revenue across regions and identifies higher- and lower-revenue regions.
- **Product revenue:** compares revenue generated by individual products.
- **Category revenue contribution:** evaluates each category's share of total revenue.
- **Monthly revenue trend:** compares revenue across the months available in the dataset.
- **Payment method:** summarizes transaction counts by payment method in the supporting analysis.

## Dashboard

The dashboard presents:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Category
- Monthly Revenue Trend

The visuals use a consistent purple theme to improve readability and maintain a unified layout.

## Key Findings

- Chattogram is the highest-revenue region, while Khulna is the lowest among the named regions in the regional summary.
- Monitor 24 inch is the highest-revenue product; Notebook Pack is the lowest-revenue product in the product summary.
- Electronics contributes approximately 68% of total revenue, indicating revenue concentration in that category.
- February is the highest-revenue month and August is the lowest-revenue month in the months shown.
- Revenue varies across regions, products, categories, and months, providing areas for further investigation.

## Recommendations

- Maintain the availability and quality of top-performing products while monitoring their revenue contribution.
- Investigate lower-performing regions and products using sales volume, customer reach, pricing, distribution, and marketing data rather than assuming a single cause.
- Evaluate opportunities to expand other categories only after checking demand, margins, and operating feasibility.
- Investigate monthly revenue fluctuations and use the findings to improve planning and inventory decisions.
- Monitor the Electronics category's share of revenue to understand and manage category concentration risk.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Data cleaning and text standardization
- Handling and documenting missing values
- Duplicate detection and removal
- Data validation and reconciliation
- Excel formulas and KPI calculations
- PivotTable-based analysis
- Chart creation and dashboard design
- Business insight development and recommendation writing

## Limitations

- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish the causes of revenue differences.
- Some missing numerical values were mathematically reconstructed and remain subject to source verification.
- Blank region records were retained because the correct region was unknown.
- Monthly reporting reflects only the months present in the dataset; missing months should not be interpreted as zero revenue.
- Revenue is not the same as profit. Cost, margin, customer-level, and marketing data would be needed for profitability or causal conclusions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates an Excel-based workflow from raw-data preparation and validation to sales analysis, dashboard reporting, and evidence-based business recommendations. It highlights patterns in revenue while identifying additional data that would be needed to investigate business performance further.
